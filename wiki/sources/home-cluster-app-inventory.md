# home-cluster app inventory (generated)

## Summary

A table of the 97 Flux Kustomizations under `kubernetes/apps` at commit `2d78212`, made with `yq` from the manifests. It lists the namespace, chart, main images, gateway, components, `dependsOn` and NetworkPolicy coverage of each one. The organized view is [[app-inventory]].

## Key points

- 97 Kustomizations in 17 namespaces. Largest: `media` (19 `ks.yaml` files) and `default` (18).
- 12 Kustomizations route to `envoy-external`. One of them is the `envoy` gateway itself.
- NetworkPolicy coverage for external apps is in [[security-posture]].

## Data and quotes

Method limits:
- "Gateway" counts only `parentRefs` that name an `envoy-*` gateway.
- "NetworkPolicy" is a text match on `owning-gateway-name` and `kube-apiserver` in the app path. Policies added by a component (for example `dragonfly`) are not counted.
- A first version of the generator missed policies written with the app-template `networkpolicies:` key. The raw file is the corrected version.

Version `2d78212` has no `default/windmill` (one of the external apps) and has new image tags. The `onepassword` Connect images moved from `docker.io/1password` to `ghcr.io/1password` (1.8.3 to 1.8.4). `atuin`, `grafana` and `grafana-instance` now also depend on `onepassword-store`, and `victoria-logs` on `rook-ceph-cluster`. Version `e36a8c8` had 98 Kustomizations and 19 in `media`, not 18 as this page said before.
